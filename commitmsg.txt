seo: serve real competitor observations from the report endpoint

/api/seo/report ignored its `type` query parameter entirely and always returned
the same keyword-centric snapshot payload. `?type=competitors` therefore showed
the 234-keyword summary rather than the 15 competitor observations the weekly
crawl had persisted, so the collected intelligence was invisible to readers.

Adds a competitor-intelligence branch backed by
seo_competitor_observations joined to seo_competitors, exposing per observation:
url, title, domain, observedAt, contentHash, market, language, evidenceType,
sourceReference, heading/FAQ/article counts and themes.

The payload deliberately carries measuredDemand, measuredRank and
measuredTraffic as explicit nulls: a competitor page proves what a competitor
targets, and nothing about demand was measured, so no such figure is emitted.

No other report type changes behaviour.
