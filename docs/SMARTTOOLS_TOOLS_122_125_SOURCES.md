# Tools 122–125 source and scope update (2026-10-26)

Tools 122–125 are deterministic training reference models for velocity, joint load, deload timing, and fiber-type indicators. They do not diagnose injury, prescribe treatment, or establish a biological fiber-type diagnosis.

Schemas were tightened to finite bounded values, trimmed labels, strict objects, and unknown-field rejection. Results remain educational indicators based on self-reported training data.
