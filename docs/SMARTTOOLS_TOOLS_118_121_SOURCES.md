# Tools 118–121 source and scope update (2026-10-26)

Tools 118–121 are deterministic training logs and reference indices. They do not diagnose CNS, predict guaranteed hypertrophy, prescribe loads, or replace coaching or medical evaluation.

Schemas were tightened to require finite bounded values, trimmed labels, strict objects, and rejection of unknown fields. Outputs remain educational indicators based on user-entered training and recovery data.
